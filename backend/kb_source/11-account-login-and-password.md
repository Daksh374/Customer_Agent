# Account, Login & Password

This article covers creating an account, signing in, OTP and password problems, changing your mobile number or email, keeping your account secure, and deleting your account.

## Creating an account

You can sign up on the website or app with:

- Your **mobile number**, verified with a one-time password (OTP), or
- **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**

After signing up, you can add your email address, name, and delivery addresses from **Account > Profile**. Each mobile number can be linked to only one account.

## Signing in

You can sign in using:

- Your mobile number and an OTP (recommended)
- Your email address and password, if you have set a password
- Google or Apple sign-in, if you signed up that way

## OTP not received

OTPs are valid for **10 minutes**. If you do not receive one:

1. Wait at least 30 seconds, then tap **Resend OTP**.
2. Check that your mobile number is entered correctly and has network coverage.
3. On dual-SIM phones, make sure the SIM with your registered number is active.
4. Check whether SMS from unknown senders is blocked or filtered by a spam app.
5. Try **Get OTP on call** or **Get OTP on WhatsApp**, if available.

You can request up to **5 OTPs per hour**. After that, please wait an hour before trying again.

## Forgot password

1. On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Open the password reset email and tap the link. The link is valid for **30 minutes** and can be used once.
4. Set a new password with at least **8 characters**, including at least one letter and one number.

If you did not receive the email, check your spam folder. You can also sign in with your mobile number and OTP instead, and then set a new password from **Account > Security > Change Password**.

## Account locked

After **5 incorrect password attempts**, your account is locked for **30 minutes** for security. You can wait, or sign in with an OTP on your registered mobile number, which works even while password sign-in is locked.

## Changing your mobile number or email

1. Go to **Account > Profile > Edit**.
2. Enter the new mobile number or email.
3. Verify with the OTP sent to your **current** mobile number or email, and then the OTP sent to the **new** one.

If you no longer have access to your old mobile number, contact support. For your security, we will verify your identity using your registered email address, a recent order ID, and the last four digits of a card used on the account, or other details. Verification takes **2 to 3 business days**.

## Managing your account

- **Addresses:** Add, edit, or delete up to 20 addresses in **Account > Saved Addresses**.
- **Saved cards and UPI:** Manage them in **Account > Payments**.
- **Notifications:** Choose which SMS, email, WhatsApp, and push notifications you receive in **Account > Notification Preferences**. Order and delivery updates cannot be turned off.
- **Name and date of birth:** Edit them in **Account > Profile**.

## Keeping your account secure

- We will never ask for your OTP, password, or UPI PIN by phone, email, or chat.
- If you notice orders you did not place, or you think someone else has access to your account, go to **Account > Security > Sign out of all devices**, change your password, and contact support immediately.
- Review devices signed in to your account in **Account > Security > Active Sessions**.

## Deleting your account

1. Go to **Account > Settings > Delete Account**.
2. Read the information shown and select a reason.
3. Confirm with the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.

Before you delete your account, please note:

- You cannot delete your account while you have **active orders, returns, exchanges, or refunds in progress**. Wait until they are completed.
- Any **Store Wallet balance** and promotional credits are **forfeited** and cannot be recovered.
- Your order history, saved addresses, and wishlist are permanently removed.
- Account deletion is completed within **7 days** and **cannot be undone**. Signing in during this period cancels the deletion request.
- Invoices and transaction records are retained for the period required by law (for example, tax laws), even after the account is deleted.

## Account suspended

We may suspend accounts involved in fraud, misuse of offers, reselling, or abuse of returns. If your account has been suspended and you believe this is a mistake, contact support with your registered mobile number.
