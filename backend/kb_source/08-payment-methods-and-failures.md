# Payment Methods & Failures

This article lists the payment methods we accept, explains EMI options, and tells you what to do if a payment fails or money is deducted without an order being placed.

## Accepted payment methods

- **UPI:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and any UPI ID. You can pay by choosing your UPI app or by entering your UPI ID and approving the collect request in your app.
- **Credit and debit cards:** Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, and American Express cards issued in India.
- **Net banking:** More than 50 Indian banks.
- **EMI:** Credit card EMI, no-cost EMI, and debit card EMI with select banks (see below).
- **Store Wallet and gift cards:** Your Store Wallet balance and gift card balance can be used alone or together with another payment method.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Pay in cash or by UPI at your doorstep on eligible orders. See the Cash on Delivery article.

All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR). We do **not** accept PayPal, cryptocurrency, cheques, demand drafts, international cards, or payments in other currencies.

## EMI options

- **Credit card EMI** is available on orders of Rs. 3,000 or more, with tenures of 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, and 24 months. Interest rates are set by your bank and shown at checkout.
- **No-cost EMI** is available on select products. The interest is given to you as an upfront discount, so the total you pay equals the product price. The bank may still charge a processing fee and GST on the interest.
- **Debit card EMI** is available for select banks for pre-approved customers. Eligibility is checked at checkout.

If you cancel or return an EMI order, the amount is refunded to your card and the bank closes the EMI. Interest and processing fees already charged may not be refunded, as per your bank's policy.

## Saved cards and security

Saved cards are stored as secure tokens in line with RBI guidelines. We never store your full card number or CVV. You can remove saved cards from **Account > Payments > Saved Cards**.

We will **never** ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, or password by phone, email, SMS, or chat. Anyone asking for these details is attempting fraud. Never share them, even if the caller claims to be from our customer support team.

## Why a payment can fail

Common reasons include:

- Wrong OTP, UPI PIN, or card details entered
- Insufficient balance or card limit
- The bank's daily transaction limit has been reached (UPI is usually limited to Rs. 1,00,000 per transaction)
- The bank's or UPI app's server is temporarily down
- The payment page was open for more than 10 minutes, or the UPI collect request was not approved within 5 minutes
- International or online transactions are disabled on your card

If a payment fails, wait a few minutes and try again, or use a different payment method.

## Money deducted but order not placed

Sometimes money is debited from your account but the order is not confirmed, usually because of a network or bank delay. In this case:

1. **Check My Orders first.** The order may be in **Payment Pending** status. We confirm pending payments with the bank within **30 minutes**. If the payment is confirmed, the order is placed automatically.
2. **If the order is not confirmed,** the amount is automatically reversed by the bank to your original payment method. You do not need to contact us.
   - UPI: usually within 48 hours, and in all cases within 5 business days
   - Cards and net banking: within 5 to 7 business days
3. **Do not pay again for the same order** unless you want to place a new order. If both payments go through, the extra payment is refunded automatically.

If the money is not back after **7 business days**, contact support with your order ID (if any), the date and amount, and the transaction ID or UPI reference number (UTR). We will raise the case with our payment gateway, and you will receive an update within 48 hours.

## Charged twice for one order

If you were charged twice for the same order, the duplicate payment is detected automatically and refunded to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days**. If it is not refunded after 7 business days, contact support with both transaction IDs.

## Paying with Store Wallet and gift cards

Your Store Wallet balance is applied at checkout when you select it. If the balance does not cover the full amount, pay the rest with another method. Gift cards are added to your Store Wallet from **Account > Gift Cards > Add Gift Card**. Store Wallet balance cannot be transferred to a bank account, except for refunds of Cash on Delivery orders, which can be sent to a bank account when the refund is requested.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change the payment method after placing an order?** No, except that a Cash on Delivery order can be converted to online payment using **Pay Now** in My Orders before it is out for delivery.

**Why was I charged a small amount like Rs. 1 or Rs. 2?** Some banks run a small verification charge when you save a card or set up UPI AutoPay. It is reversed automatically.

**Is it safe to pay online?** Yes. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways with encryption and two-factor authentication.
