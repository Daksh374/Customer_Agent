# Order Cancellation

This article explains when and how you can cancel an order, what happens to your money, and what to do if an order can no longer be cancelled.

## Cancellation window

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time **before it is shipped**. The cancellation option is available while the order status is **Order Placed**, **Confirmed**, or **Packed**. Once the status changes to **Shipped**, the order can no longer be cancelled online.

There is no cancellation fee for orders cancelled before shipping. Any delivery fee or Cash on Delivery (COD) fee you paid is refunded in full.

## How to cancel an order

1. Open the website or app and go to **Account > My Orders**.
2. Select the order you want to cancel.
3. Tap **Cancel Order** (or **Cancel Item** to cancel only one product).
4. Choose a reason for cancellation. This helps us improve, and it is required.
5. Confirm the cancellation.

You will receive a cancellation confirmation by SMS and email within a few minutes. If you do not see the Cancel button, the order has already been shipped or the item is non-cancellable.

## Cancelling part of an order

If your order contains several items, you can cancel individual items before they are shipped. The remaining items will be delivered as usual. If cancelling an item brings your order below the free-delivery threshold of Rs. 499, the delivery fee is not charged again after the order is placed; you keep free delivery on the remaining items.

If a coupon was applied to the order, cancelling one item may reduce the refund for that item, because the discount is shared proportionally across all items in the order. See the Coupons & Discounts article for details.

## Cancellation after shipping

Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You still have two options:

- **Refuse the delivery.** When the delivery agent arrives, tell them you do not want to accept the package. The package goes back to our warehouse. For prepaid orders, the product price is refunded once the package reaches the warehouse, which usually takes 7 to 10 days after the refusal. The delivery fee, if any, is not refunded for refused deliveries.
- **Accept the delivery and return it.** If the product is eligible for return, you can raise a return request from **My Orders** within the return window. See the Returns & Refunds article.

Please note that refusing Cash on Delivery orders repeatedly can lead to COD being disabled on your account. See the Cash on Delivery article.

## Items that cannot be cancelled

Some items cannot be cancelled once the order is confirmed:

- Personalised or customised products (for example, engraved items or custom-printed clothing) cannot be cancelled after 24 hours from order placement, because production starts after that.
- Gift cards and e-vouchers cannot be cancelled once they have been sent to the recipient.
- Digital products such as software licence keys cannot be cancelled once the key has been delivered.

The product page clearly shows if an item is non-cancellable.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

For prepaid orders, the refund is started automatically as soon as the cancellation is confirmed. The money goes back to the original payment method:

- Store Wallet: within 2 hours
- UPI: 1 to 3 business days
- Debit card, credit card, and net banking: 5 to 7 business days

For Cash on Delivery orders, there is nothing to refund because no payment was collected.

Refund timelines depend on your bank once we have processed the refund. You will receive a refund reference number (ARN or RRN) by email, which you can share with your bank if the money does not appear on time.

## Cancellations made by us

Occasionally we may have to cancel an order ourselves. Common reasons include:

- The product went out of stock after the order was placed.
- The delivery address is not serviceable by our courier partners.
- The product was listed at an incorrect price because of a technical error.
- The order failed a payment or fraud verification check.
- A Cash on Delivery order was not confirmed within 24 hours when confirmation was required.

If we cancel your order, you will be informed by SMS and email, and any amount you paid is refunded in full to the original payment method. You will not be charged any fee.

## Pre-orders

Pre-order items can be cancelled at any time before they are shipped, with a full refund, even if the release date has been announced.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change the product, size, or colour instead of cancelling?** An order cannot be edited after it is placed. Cancel the item before it ships and place a new order with the correct product.

**Can I cancel after the order is out for delivery?** No. You can refuse the delivery at the doorstep or return the item after delivery if it is eligible.

**Will cancelling affect my coupon?** If you cancel the whole order before shipping and the coupon is still valid, the coupon is restored to your account and can be used again.
