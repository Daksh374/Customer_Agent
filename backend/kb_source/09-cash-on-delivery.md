# Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD), also called Pay on Delivery, lets you pay when your order arrives. This article explains COD availability, limits, fees, verification, and refunds.

## COD availability

COD is available on most products for most serviceable pin codes. To check, enter your pin code on the product page and look for "Cash on Delivery available", or check the payment options at checkout.

COD is **not available** for:

- Orders above **Rs. 50,000**
- Gift cards and e-vouchers
- Gold coins, silver coins, and precious jewellery
- Pre-order items and some high-demand product launches
- Orders shipped with Same-Day Delivery
- Accounts where COD has been disabled (see below)

## COD fee

A convenience fee of **Rs. 20** is charged per COD order. The fee is shown at checkout and included in the amount you pay at delivery. It is refunded if the order is cancelled before shipping or if you convert the order to online payment using Pay Now.

## How to pay at delivery

You can pay the delivery agent by:

- **Cash**, or
- **UPI**, by scanning the QR code shown by the delivery agent or on the delivery SMS link

Card payments at the doorstep are not supported. Please keep the exact amount ready if paying in cash, as delivery agents carry limited change. You will receive a payment confirmation SMS once the payment is recorded.

You cannot open the package before paying, except for items delivered with **Open Box Delivery**, where the agent lets you check the product before completing delivery. Open Box Delivery is shown on the product page when available.

## COD order verification

For your first COD order, and for COD orders above **Rs. 5,000**, we may ask you to confirm the order. You will receive an SMS or WhatsApp message with a confirmation link, or an automated call asking you to press 1 to confirm.

If the order is not confirmed within **24 hours**, it is cancelled automatically. You can place the order again at any time.

## Converting COD to online payment

You can pay online for a COD order at any time before it is out for delivery. Go to **My Orders > select the order > Pay Now** and pay by UPI, card, or net banking. The Rs. 20 COD fee is removed when you pay online.

## When COD may be disabled

To keep COD available for everyone, COD may be disabled on an account if:

- **3 or more COD orders** are refused at the doorstep or not accepted within 6 months, or
- COD orders are frequently cancelled after being shipped, or
- The account is linked to unusual or fraudulent activity.

If COD is disabled, you can still order using any online payment method. COD is usually restored automatically after 6 months. You can contact support if you believe COD was disabled by mistake.

## Returns and refunds for COD orders

Because no card or bank account is linked to a COD payment, refunds for returned COD orders are sent to:

- Your **Store Wallet**, within 2 hours of the refund being started, or
- Your **bank account through NEFT**, within 3 to 5 business days. Add your account holder name, account number, and IFSC code when you raise the return request.

Please double-check your bank details. Refunds sent to an incorrect account number cannot be recovered by us.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is COD not showing for my order?** The order value may be above Rs. 50,000, the cart may contain an item that is not eligible for COD, COD may not be available at your pin code, or COD may be disabled on your account.

**Can I pay part of a COD order with my Store Wallet?** Yes. Select Store Wallet at checkout and choose COD for the remaining amount.

**The delivery agent asked for more money than shown in the app.** Pay only the amount shown in My Orders and in the Out for Delivery SMS. If an agent asks for more, do not pay and report it to support immediately.
