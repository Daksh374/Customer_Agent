# Shipping & Delivery

This article covers delivery timelines, shipping charges, delivery areas, delivery attempts, and what happens when a delivery fails.

## Delivery areas

We deliver to more than 19,000 pin codes across India. To check whether we deliver to your location, enter your pin code on any product page. The page will show whether delivery is available and the expected delivery date.

We currently do not ship outside India. We also do not deliver to PO Box addresses, military APO/FPO addresses, or hotel addresses without a guest name.

## Delivery options and timelines

Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays) from the day the order is shipped.

**Standard Delivery**
- Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad): 2 to 4 business days
- Other cities and towns: 4 to 7 business days
- Remote areas (North-Eastern states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, and some hilly regions): 7 to 12 business days

**Express Delivery**
- Available in 100+ cities for select pin codes and products
- Delivered in 1 to 2 business days
- Shown as an option at checkout only if your pin code and all items in the cart are eligible

**Same-Day Delivery**
- Available in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune for select products
- Order before 12 PM to receive it the same day by 10 PM
- Orders placed after 12 PM are delivered the next day

**Scheduled Delivery for large items**
- Furniture, large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, televisions above 43 inches), and other bulky items are delivered on a scheduled date
- Delivery takes 5 to 10 business days, and you can choose a delivery slot at checkout or when our team calls you
- Installation for eligible appliances is scheduled within 48 hours of delivery

## Shipping charges

- **Standard Delivery:** Free on orders of Rs. 499 or more. Orders below Rs. 499 have a delivery fee of Rs. 49.
- **Express Delivery:** Rs. 99 per order, regardless of order value.
- **Same-Day Delivery:** Rs. 149 per order.
- **Cash on Delivery fee:** Rs. 20 per order, in addition to any delivery fee. See the Cash on Delivery article.
- **Large items:** Delivery is free for furniture and large appliances. Installation is free where the product page says "Free installation"; otherwise installation charges are shown on the product page.

The order value for free delivery is calculated after discounts and coupons, and does not include the delivery fee itself.

## Delivery days and timing

Standard deliveries happen from Monday to Saturday between 9 AM and 9 PM. Express and Same-Day deliveries may also happen on Sundays in select cities. You will receive an SMS when your order is Out for Delivery, including the delivery agent's contact number.

## Delivery attempts and failed delivery

The courier makes **up to 3 delivery attempts** on consecutive working days. A delivery attempt can fail if:

- Nobody is available at the address
- The phone number is switched off or unreachable
- The address is incomplete or cannot be found
- The customer refuses the package

If all 3 attempts fail, the package is **Returned to Origin (RTO)** and the order is cancelled. For prepaid orders, the product price is refunded to the original payment method after the package reaches our warehouse (usually 7 to 10 days). The delivery fee is not refunded when delivery fails because the customer was unavailable or refused the package.

You can reschedule a delivery by replying to the Delivery Attempted SMS or by calling the delivery agent.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address from **My Orders > Order details > Change address** while the order is in Order Placed, Confirmed, or Packed status, provided the new address is serviceable. Once the order is shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Getting the address right

To avoid delays, include your house or flat number, building name, street, landmark, city, pin code, and a phone number that will be reachable on the delivery day. You can save up to 20 addresses in **Account > Saved Addresses**.

## Delivering to someone else

You can send an order to a friend or family member by adding their name, address, and phone number at checkout. The delivery OTP (for orders above Rs. 5,000) is sent to the phone number on the delivery address. You can also add gift wrapping on eligible items; see the General Customer Support FAQ.
