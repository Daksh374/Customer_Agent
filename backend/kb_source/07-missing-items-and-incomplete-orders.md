# Missing Items & Incomplete Orders

If your package is missing a product, an accessory, or a free gift, this article explains what to check first and how to report it.

## Before you report: common reasons an item seems missing

**1. Your order was shipped in multiple packages.**
Orders with several items are often shipped separately from different warehouses or sellers, and the packages may arrive on different days. Go to **Account > My Orders**, open the order, and check how many shipments it has. Each shipment has its own tracking ID and delivery date.

**2. The accessory is not included with the product.**
Some products do not come with every accessory. For example, many smartphones are sold without a charger or earphones, and some laptops do not include a mouse. Check the **In the box** section on the product page to see exactly what is included.

**3. The free gift ships separately.**
Free gifts that come with an offer are sometimes shipped in a separate package, up to 7 days after the main product. The free gift appears as a separate item in My Orders.

**4. Part of the order was cancelled.**
If an item went out of stock, we may cancel that item and refund it. Check your email and SMS for a cancellation message.

**5. The item is inside other packaging.**
Small items are sometimes packed inside a larger box or wrapped in protective material. Please check all packaging carefully before discarding it.

## Time limit for reporting

Report a missing item within **48 hours** of the package being delivered. Reports made after 48 hours may not be accepted because we cannot verify the package condition with the courier.

## How to report a missing item

1. Go to **Account > My Orders** and select the order.
2. Tap **Help > Missing item or accessory**.
3. Select the missing product, accessory, or free gift.
4. Upload the proof listed below and describe what was missing.
5. Submit the request. You will receive a ticket ID by SMS and email.

## Proof required

- Photos of the outer package from all sides, including the shipping label
- Photos of the inner packaging and the items you did receive
- An unboxing video, if you recorded one. It is **mandatory** for mobiles, laptops, tablets, cameras, and any order above Rs. 10,000.
- The weight of the package, if you are able to weigh it

## Investigation

We compare the package weight recorded at our warehouse with the weight recorded by the courier at each hub, and review packing records and your proof. The investigation takes **3 to 5 business days**. We will update you by email and SMS.

## Resolution

If the investigation confirms that an item was missing, you can choose:

- **Reshipment:** The missing item is sent to you free of charge, usually within 3 to 5 business days, or
- **Refund:** The price you paid for the missing item is refunded to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, it is refunded to your Store Wallet or bank account.

If the missing item was a free gift that is no longer in stock, we will offer Store Wallet credit equal to the gift's listed value.

## Empty or tampered packages

If you receive a package that is empty, or the seal or tape looks tampered with, do not accept it if the delivery agent is still present. If you have already accepted it, report it within 48 hours with photos and the unboxing video. These cases are treated as high priority and investigated within 3 business days.

## Frequently asked questions

**My order shows Delivered but only some items arrived.** Check whether the other items are in a separate shipment with a different tracking ID. If all shipments show Delivered and an item is still missing, report it as described above.

**Can I report a missing item for a Cash on Delivery order?** Yes. The process is the same. Refunds for COD orders go to your Store Wallet or bank account.

**The warranty card or user manual is missing.** Most brands provide digital manuals on their website. Your tax invoice, which you can download from My Orders, acts as proof of purchase for warranty claims, so a missing warranty card does not affect your warranty.
