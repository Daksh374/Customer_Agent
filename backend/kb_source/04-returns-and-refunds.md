# Returns & Refunds

This article explains which products can be returned, the return window for each category, how to raise a return, and when and how you receive your refund.

## Return windows by category

The return window starts on the date the item is delivered.

- **Fashion** (clothing, shoes and other footwear, bags, watches, and accessories): 10 days. Return or exchange.
- **Home, kitchen, furniture, and home decor:** 7 days. Return.
- **Books, toys, stationery, and sports equipment:** 7 days. Return.
- **Beauty, personal care, grooming, and health products:** 7 days. Returnable only if the product is unopened with its seal intact, or if it arrived damaged, expired, or wrong.
- **Mobiles, laptops, tablets, cameras, and other electronics:** 7 days. **Replacement only**, and only if the item is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted for electronics.
- **Large appliances** (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, televisions): 10 days. Replacement only, after a technician confirms the defect.

The exact return policy for each product is also shown on the product page under **Return Policy**.

## Non-returnable items

The following items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged, defective, or wrong:

- Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear, and socks
- Personalised or customised products
- Gift cards and e-vouchers
- Groceries and perishable items
- Digital products and software licence keys
- Opened beauty and personal care products
- Items marked "Non-returnable" on the product page

## Return conditions

For a return to be accepted, the item must be:

- Unused, unwashed, and in the same condition in which it was delivered
- In its original packaging, including the brand box for footwear, watches, and electronics
- Returned with all original tags, labels, accessories, manuals, and warranty cards
- Returned together with any free gift that came with it; if the free gift is not returned, its value is deducted from the refund

## How to raise a return

1. Go to **Account > My Orders** and select the item.
2. Tap **Return** and choose a reason.
3. Upload photos if the item is damaged, defective, or wrong.
4. Confirm the pickup address and choose a pickup slot.
5. Choose your refund method (see below) and submit.

You will receive a return request ID by SMS and email.

## Pickup

A courier will pick up the item within **1 to 3 business days**. The courier makes up to 2 pickup attempts. For fashion items, the pickup agent may check the item at your doorstep, for example that the tags are intact and the product is unused. Please keep the item packed and ready.

If reverse pickup is not available at your pin code, you will be asked to self-ship the item to our returns centre within 5 days. We reimburse self-shipping costs of up to Rs. 100 as Store Wallet credit after the item is received.

## When the refund is started

- If the item passes the quality check at the doorstep, the refund is started immediately after pickup.
- If the item is checked at our warehouse, the refund is started within 2 business days after the item reaches the warehouse.

## Refund timelines

After the refund is started, it reaches you in:

- **Store Wallet:** within 2 hours
- **UPI:** 1 to 3 business days
- **Debit card, credit card, and net banking:** 5 to 7 business days
- **EMI orders:** refunded to the card in 5 to 7 business days; the bank then cancels the EMI. Interest or processing fees already charged by the bank may not be refunded, as per your bank's terms.
- **Cash on Delivery orders:** to your Store Wallet (within 2 hours) or to your bank account through NEFT (3 to 5 business days). To receive a bank refund, add your account number and IFSC code when you raise the return.

When the refund is processed, you receive a reference number (ARN or RRN) by email. If the money is not visible after the timeline above, share this reference number with your bank.

## How much is refunded

You receive the amount you actually paid for the item. If a coupon or bank offer was applied to the order, the discount is divided proportionally across all items, so the refund for one item reflects its share of the discount. The delivery fee is refunded only if the whole order is returned because of our error, such as a damaged, defective, wrong, or missing item.

## Rejected returns

If the returned item fails the quality check (for example, it is used, damaged by the customer, or missing tags or accessories), the return is rejected and the item is sent back to you. You will receive photos and the reason by email. If you disagree with the decision, contact support within 48 hours.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I return an item after the return window?** No. After the window closes, defective products are covered by the manufacturer's warranty; see the Warranty & Replacement article.

**Can I return only one item from my order?** Yes. Each item has its own return option.

**Can I get a different product instead of a refund?** Only size or colour exchanges of the same product are supported (see Product Exchange). For a different product, return the item and place a new order.
