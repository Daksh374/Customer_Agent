# Damaged, Defective or Wrong Product

We are sorry if your order did not arrive in perfect condition. This article explains how to report a damaged, defective, or wrong product, what proof you need, and how quickly it will be resolved.

## What counts as damaged, defective, or wrong

- **Damaged:** The product is broken, cracked, dented, torn, stained, or leaking when it arrives, usually because of damage during transit.
- **Defective:** The product arrives looking fine but does not work as it should, for example a phone that does not switch on, a mixer with a faulty motor, or a zipper that does not close.
- **Wrong product:** You received a different product, size, colour, model, or quantity from what you ordered.

## Time limits for reporting

- **Damaged or wrong product:** Report within **48 hours** of delivery.
- **Defective product:** Report within the return window for the product's category: 7 days for electronics and most other categories, 10 days for fashion and large appliances.

After the return window, defects are handled under the manufacturer's warranty. See the Warranty & Replacement article.

## Check the package at delivery

If the outer package is torn, crushed, wet, or looks opened or tampered with, you can refuse to accept it at the doorstep. The package will be sent back and a replacement or refund will be arranged. If you accept a package that looks damaged, take clear photos of the outer box and the shipping label before opening it.

## How to report the problem

1. Go to **Account > My Orders** and select the item.
2. Tap **Return** or **Replace** and choose the reason: Damaged, Defective, or Wrong item received.
3. Upload clear photos (see below) and describe the issue.
4. Choose whether you want a replacement or a refund.
5. Confirm the pickup address and submit.

## Proof required

To help us resolve your claim quickly, please provide:

- 3 to 5 clear photos showing the product, the damage or defect, the outer box, and the shipping label
- For wrong products, a photo of the product you received next to its label or barcode
- An **unboxing video** is **mandatory** for mobiles, laptops, tablets, and cameras, and for any order above Rs. 10,000, when you are reporting physical damage or a wrong or missing item. The video should start before the sealed package is opened and show the shipping label clearly.

We strongly recommend recording an unboxing video for all high-value purchases.

## Technician verification

For large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, televisions) and some electronics, a technician visit is required to confirm the defect. The brand or our service partner will visit within **48 to 72 hours** of your request. For some mobile phone brands, you may be asked to get a Dead on Arrival (DOA) certificate from the brand's authorised service centre within the replacement window. The support team will tell you if this applies.

## Resolution options

- **Replacement:** You receive the same product (same model, size, and colour) free of charge. The damaged, defective, or wrong item is picked up when the replacement is delivered, or before it for large items. Replacements are usually completed within **5 to 7 business days**.
- **Refund:** If a replacement is not available because the product is out of stock, or if you prefer a refund for a damaged or wrong item, you receive a full refund including any delivery fee you paid. Refund timelines are listed in the Returns & Refunds article.

For electronics, the default resolution is a replacement. A refund is given only if a replacement is not possible.

## Wrong product received

Please do not use, wash, or remove the tags from a wrong product, and do not open its seal if it is sealed. The wrong item must be returned unused. If you received the correct product but in the wrong size, that is also treated as a wrong product, and the correct size is sent free of charge.

## Expired products

If a food, beauty, or health product is expired or has less than the minimum shelf life shown on the product page, report it within 48 hours with a photo of the expiry date. You will receive a full refund or a replacement.

## Frequently asked questions

**I threw away the box. Can I still get a replacement?** Please contact support. For electronics, the original box is usually required, but we review each case.

**What if the replacement is also defective?** Report it within 48 hours of receiving the replacement. You will be offered a full refund.

**Do I have to pay for the return pickup?** No. Pickups for damaged, defective, and wrong products are always free.
