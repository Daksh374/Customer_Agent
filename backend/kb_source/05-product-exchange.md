# Product Exchange

An exchange lets you swap an item for a different size or colour of the same product, without paying again and without waiting for a refund. This article explains eligibility, the exchange process, and what to do if an exchange is not available.

## Which products can be exchanged

Exchanges are available for **fashion products**: clothing, shoes and other footwear, bags, and fashion accessories. The exchange window is **10 days from delivery**, the same as the return window for fashion.

An exchange is possible only for:

- The **same product** in a **different size or colour**
- A variant with the **same price** as the item you bought

Exchanges are not available for:

- Electronics, appliances, home products, beauty products, and books (for damaged or defective items in these categories, a replacement is offered instead; see the Damaged, Defective or Wrong Product article)
- Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear, and socks
- Items bought during a "Final Sale" or clearance sale
- Items bought as part of a combo or bundle offer
- Pin codes where exchange pickup is not supported (you can return the item instead)

Each item can be exchanged **only once**. If the exchanged item also does not fit, you can return it within the original return window.

## Condition of the item

The item you are exchanging must meet the same conditions as a return: unused, unwashed, with all original tags and labels attached, and in its original packaging (including the shoe box for footwear).

## How to request an exchange

1. Go to **Account > My Orders** and select the item.
2. Tap **Exchange**. If you only see Return, the item or your pin code is not eligible for exchange.
3. Choose the new size or colour. Only variants that are in stock and have the same price are shown.
4. Confirm your address and submit the request.

You will receive an exchange request ID by SMS and email.

## How the exchange is delivered

Exchanges work as a **doorstep swap**. The delivery agent brings the new item and collects the old item at the same time. Please keep the old item packed with its tags and ready to hand over. The agent will check that the old item is unused and has its tags before handing over the new item. If the old item fails this check, the exchange is cancelled and the new item goes back with the agent.

The new item is usually delivered within **3 to 7 business days** of the exchange request, depending on your location.

## Charges

Exchanges are **free**. There is no delivery fee or pickup fee for an exchange, and Cash on Delivery orders are also eligible for exchange at no extra cost.

## When your size or colour is out of stock

If the size or colour you want is out of stock, it will not appear in the exchange options. You can:

- Tap **Notify Me** on the product page to get an alert when it is back in stock, as long as your exchange window is still open, or
- Return the item for a refund and order a different product.

## Price differences

Exchanges are only for variants at the same price. If you want a variant with a different price, or a completely different product, return the original item for a refund and place a new order. Coupons used on the original order cannot be transferred to the new order.

## Replacement versus exchange

- An **exchange** is for fashion items that do not fit or where you would prefer another colour. You choose a different size or colour.
- A **replacement** is for items that arrived damaged, defective, or wrong. You receive the same product again, in the same size and colour, free of charge. Replacements are available across all categories within the category's return window.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I exchange an item bought with a coupon?** Yes. The exchange is for the same product at the same price, so your original discount stays unchanged.

**Can I exchange a gift I received?** The exchange must be requested from the account that placed the order. Ask the person who ordered it to raise the request, or contact support with the order ID.

**The exchange pickup agent did not come. What should I do?** The courier makes up to 2 attempts. If both are missed, the exchange request is cancelled and you can raise a new request if you are still within the 10-day window.
