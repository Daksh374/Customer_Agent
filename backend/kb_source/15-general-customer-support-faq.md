# General Customer Support FAQ

This article answers common questions that are not covered by the other help articles, including how to contact us, support hours, complaints, gift orders, bulk orders, and safety.

## Customer care timings and how to contact us

Customer care is open every day. Our human support agents work from **8 AM to 10 PM IST, all 7 days** a week, and the chat assistant is available 24 hours a day. You can reach us in these ways:

- **Chat assistant:** Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, from **Account > Help Center > Chat with us**. The assistant answers common questions instantly and can connect you to a human agent.
- **Human support agents:** Available by chat from **8 AM to 10 PM IST, all 7 days**, including Sundays and most public holidays.
- **Email tickets:** Raise a ticket from **Help Center > Raise a Ticket**. We reply within **24 hours**.
- **Callback:** Request a callback from the Help Center during support hours. A support agent calls you back, usually within 2 hours.

For faster help, keep your order ID ready. You can find it in **My Orders** and in your order confirmation email.

## Support tickets and response times

Every request you raise gets a **ticket ID**, which is sent by SMS and email. You can see the status of all your tickets in **Help Center > My Tickets**. Most tickets are resolved within **48 hours**. Tickets that need investigation with a courier, seller, or bank may take up to 5 to 7 business days, and you will receive updates along the way.

## Escalations and grievances

If your issue is not resolved to your satisfaction, you can ask the support agent to escalate your ticket to a supervisor.

If the issue is still unresolved, you can contact our **Grievance Officer**, appointed as per the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. Go to **Help Center > Grievance Redressal** and submit your complaint with your ticket ID. Grievances are acknowledged within **48 hours** and resolved within **30 days**.

## Gift orders and gift wrapping

- You can send an order to someone else by entering their name, address, and phone number at checkout.
- **Gift wrapping** is available on eligible products for **Rs. 30** per item. You can add a personal gift message of up to 200 characters.
- For gift-wrapped orders, the invoice is not placed inside the package, so the price is not visible to the recipient. The invoice is sent to your email.
- Returns and exchanges for gift orders must be requested from the account that placed the order.

## Changing an order after placing it

Orders cannot be edited after they are placed. You cannot add items, remove items (other than by cancelling them), change the size or colour, or merge two orders. To make changes, cancel the item before it is shipped and place a new order. The delivery address can be changed before shipping, as explained in the Shipping & Delivery article.

## Bulk and corporate orders

For orders of **10 or more units** of the same product, or for corporate gifting, go to **Help Center > Bulk Orders** and submit your requirements. Our bulk orders team replies within 2 business days with pricing, GST invoicing, and delivery timelines.

## Store Wallet

The Store Wallet holds refund credits, gift card balances, and promotional credits. You can see your balance and transaction history in **Account > Store Wallet**. Refund credits never expire, while promotional credits have an expiry date. Wallet balance cannot be transferred to another account or withdrawn to a bank account.

## Staying safe from fraud

- We never ask for your OTP, password, UPI PIN, card PIN, or CVV.
- We never ask you to install screen-sharing or remote-access apps.
- We do not run lucky draws, prize schemes, or job offers over phone calls or WhatsApp messages.
- Our support team will never ask you to pay money to receive a refund.
- Official messages come only from our verified SMS sender IDs, our verified WhatsApp business account, and our official email domain.

If you receive a suspicious call or message, do not share any details. Report it from **Help Center > Report Fraud**.

## Privacy and communication preferences

We use your personal information only to process orders, deliver products, provide support, and, if you agree, send you offers. You can manage marketing emails, SMS, and notifications in **Account > Notification Preferences**, or tap Unsubscribe in any marketing email. Order and delivery updates are always sent.

## Languages

Customer support is available in **English and Hindi**. The chat assistant currently answers in English.

## Feedback

After a ticket is resolved, you will receive a short survey asking about your experience. Your feedback helps us improve our service and support.
