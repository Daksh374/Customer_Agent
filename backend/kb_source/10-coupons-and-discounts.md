# Coupons & Discounts

This article explains the types of offers available, the rules for using coupons, why a coupon may not work, and how discounts are handled when you cancel or return an order.

## Types of offers

- **Coupon codes:** Codes you enter at checkout, such as festive sale codes or brand coupons.
- **Auto-applied offers:** Discounts shown on the product page and applied automatically, such as "Buy 2, get 10% off".
- **Bank offers:** Instant discounts or cashback when you pay with a specific bank's card or with UPI. These are applied on the payment page.
- **First-order offer:** New customers get 10% off, up to Rs. 150, on their first order of Rs. 499 or more, using the code **WELCOME10**.
- **Store Wallet credits:** Promotional credits added to your Store Wallet, which can be used at checkout.

All available coupons for your cart are listed on the checkout page under **Apply Coupon**.

## Coupon rules

- Only **one coupon code** can be used per order.
- A coupon code **can be combined** with a bank offer and with auto-applied product offers, unless the coupon's terms say otherwise.
- Each coupon has a **minimum order value**. It is calculated on the cart value after product discounts and before the delivery fee.
- Many coupons have a **maximum discount cap**, for example "20% off up to Rs. 300".
- Coupons may be limited to certain **categories, brands, or products**.
- Coupons have a **validity period** and expire at 11:59 PM on the last valid date.
- Some coupons can be used only **once per account**, only by **new customers**, only on the **app**, or only with a specific **payment method**.

The complete terms for each coupon are shown by tapping **T&C** next to the coupon at checkout.

## Why a coupon may not work

If you see "Coupon not applicable", check the following:

1. **The coupon has expired.** Check the validity date in the coupon's terms.
2. **The minimum order value is not met.** Remember that the value is calculated after other discounts.
3. **Some items in your cart are excluded.** For example, a fashion coupon will not apply to electronics in the same cart.
4. **You have already used it.** Single-use coupons cannot be used twice on the same account.
5. **It is for a specific user group,** such as new customers or users who received it by email.
6. **It is valid only on the app** or only with a particular payment method.
7. **The code was typed incorrectly.** Codes are not case-sensitive, but spaces and extra characters will make them invalid.

Expired coupons cannot be extended, reactivated, or reissued.

## Bank offers and cashback

Bank instant discounts are applied on the payment page when you pay with the eligible card or UPI app. If the discount does not appear, check the offer's minimum transaction amount and whether your card type (credit, debit, or EMI) is eligible.

Cashback offers are credited by the bank or UPI app, not by us, usually within **90 days**, according to the bank's terms. For cashback questions, please contact your bank or UPI app.

## Discounts when you cancel or return

- **Cancelling the whole order before shipping:** A coupon that is still valid is restored to your account and can be used again. Expired coupons are not restored.
- **Cancelling or returning part of an order:** The coupon discount is divided proportionally across all items in the order. The refund for each item is the amount you actually paid for it after its share of the discount.
- **Returns after delivery:** Coupons are not restored after a return.
- **Bank offers:** If you return part of an order, the bank discount is also adjusted proportionally in the refund.

**Example:** You buy a shirt for Rs. 1,000 and trousers for Rs. 1,000, and apply a coupon for Rs. 200 off. Each item carries Rs. 100 of the discount. If you return the shirt, you receive Rs. 900.

## Price changes after you order

Prices and offers can change at any time. We do not offer price protection, so if the price of a product drops after you have placed your order, the difference is not refunded. You can cancel the order before it ships and place a new order at the lower price.

## Store Wallet credits

- **Refund credits** (money refunded to your wallet) never expire.
- **Promotional credits** have an expiry date, which is shown in **Account > Store Wallet**. Promotional credits cannot be used on gift cards and may be limited to a maximum amount per order.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I apply a coupon after placing the order?** No. Coupons must be applied at checkout before payment.

**I forgot to apply a coupon. Can I get the discount?** Cancel the order before it ships and place a new order with the coupon.

**Why is the discount smaller than I expected?** The coupon may have a maximum discount cap, or some items in your cart may not be eligible.
