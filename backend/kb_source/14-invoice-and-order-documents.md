# Invoice & Order Documents

This article explains how to download your invoice, how to get a GST invoice for business purchases, and which other order documents are available.

## Downloading your invoice

A tax invoice is generated when your order is **shipped**. To download it:

1. Go to **Account > My Orders**.
2. Select the order.
3. Tap **Download Invoice**. The invoice downloads as a PDF.

If your order was shipped in multiple packages, or contains items from different sellers, you will have **one invoice per shipment or seller**. Each invoice can be downloaded separately from the order details page.

The invoice is not always included inside the package, because many orders are shipped paperless. You can always download it from My Orders. Invoices remain available for download for at least **3 years** from the order date.

## What the invoice contains

- Invoice number and date
- Seller name, address, and GSTIN
- Billing and shipping address
- Product description, HSN code, quantity, and price
- GST breakdown (CGST and SGST, or IGST for inter-state orders)
- Discounts applied, delivery fee, and COD fee, if any
- Total amount paid

## GST invoice for business purchases

If you are buying for a registered business and want to claim input tax credit:

1. At checkout, tap **Add GST details** (or **Use GST invoice**).
2. Enter your **GSTIN** and your **registered business name** exactly as they appear on your GST registration.
3. Place the order.

Please note:

- GST details **must be added before placing the order**. They cannot be added or changed after the order is placed, because the invoice is generated with the details provided at checkout.
- The GSTIN must be active and valid; it is verified automatically at checkout.
- The GST invoice is issued in the name of the registered business, and your GSTIN appears on the invoice.
- Some sellers and product categories may not support GST invoices; this is shown at checkout.

## Name or address errors on the invoice

The invoice uses the billing name and address entered at checkout. It cannot be edited after it is generated. If the invoice contains an error made by us or the seller (for example, a wrong price or a wrong GSTIN when you entered the correct one), contact support within **30 days** of delivery, and we will arrange a corrected invoice.

## Other order documents

- **Order confirmation:** Sent by email and SMS when the order is placed. It lists the items, prices, delivery address, and payment method, and can be used as an order receipt.
- **Payment receipt:** Available in **My Orders > Order details > Payment details**, showing the payment method, transaction ID, and amount.
- **Credit note:** When an order is cancelled or returned after the invoice has been generated, a credit note is issued for the refunded amount. It can be downloaded from the order details page after the refund is processed.
- **Refund confirmation:** An email with the refund amount, method, and bank reference number (ARN or RRN).
- **Warranty documents:** The tax invoice serves as proof of purchase for warranty claims. Protection plan certificates are available in **Account > My Protection Plans**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why can't I see the Download Invoice button?** The invoice is generated only after the order is shipped. For cancelled orders that were never shipped, no invoice is generated.

**The invoice amount is different from what I paid.** The invoice may be split by seller or shipment. The Store Wallet amount and bank discounts may also be shown separately. Add up all invoices for the order, or check the payment details in My Orders.

**Can I get an invoice for a gift order without the price?** When you choose gift wrapping, the invoice is not placed inside the package; it is sent only to your email and remains available in My Orders.

**Do you provide a GST invoice for Cash on Delivery orders?** Yes, as long as the GST details were added at checkout.
