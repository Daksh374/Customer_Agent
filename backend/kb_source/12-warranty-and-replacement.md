# Warranty & Replacement

This article explains how product warranties work, how to make a warranty claim, and how extended warranty and protection plans work.

## Replacement window versus warranty

- **During the replacement window** (7 days from delivery for most electronics, 10 days for large appliances), we handle damaged, defective, or wrong products directly. Raise a request from **My Orders**. See the Damaged, Defective or Wrong Product article.
- **After the replacement window**, defects are covered by the **manufacturer's warranty** and are handled by the brand's authorised service centre.

## Manufacturer warranty

Most electronics and appliances come with a warranty from the brand. The warranty period is shown on the product page under **Warranty**. Typical periods are:

- Mobiles, tablets, laptops, and most electronics: 1 year
- Large appliances: 1 to 2 years on the product, and often longer (for example, 5 to 10 years) on the compressor or motor
- Accessories such as chargers and cables: 6 months to 1 year

The warranty starts on the date of delivery. Warranty terms are set by the brand, not by us.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. **Download your tax invoice** from **My Orders > Order details > Download Invoice**. The invoice is your proof of purchase.
2. **Find the brand's service centre or customer care number.** These are listed on the product page under Warranty, on the warranty card, or on the brand's website.
3. **Contact the brand** and share the invoice, the product's serial number or IMEI, and a description of the problem.
4. The brand will repair or replace the product, as per its warranty policy.

The brand decides whether a product is repaired or replaced under warranty. We cannot override a brand's warranty decision, but we can help you contact the brand if you face difficulties.

## What the warranty usually does not cover

- Physical damage, such as cracked screens, dents, or broken parts caused after delivery
- Liquid or water damage
- Repairs or opening of the product by an unauthorised person
- Products with a tampered or missing serial number
- Consumable parts such as batteries after 6 months, bulbs, and filters, unless the brand says otherwise
- Cosmetic damage and normal wear and tear
- Software issues caused by unofficial software or modifications

## Extended warranty and protection plans

You can buy a protection plan for eligible products at checkout, or within **7 days of delivery** from **My Orders > Add Protection Plan**.

- **Extended Warranty:** Extends the manufacturer's warranty by 1 or 2 years. It covers the same manufacturing defects as the original warranty and starts when the manufacturer's warranty ends.
- **Damage Protection:** Covers accidental physical damage and liquid damage for 1 year from delivery. One claim is allowed during the plan period.

Protection plans are provided by our insurance and service partner. The plan certificate is emailed to you and is available in **Account > My Protection Plans**.

## Making a protection plan claim

1. Go to **Account > My Protection Plans** and select the plan.
2. Tap **Raise a Claim** and describe the problem.
3. A doorstep pickup or technician visit is arranged within **48 to 72 hours**.
4. Repairs are usually completed within **7 to 10 business days**.
5. If the product cannot be repaired, you receive a replacement or a payout based on the plan terms.

A small processing fee may apply to Damage Protection claims; the amount is shown in your plan certificate.

## Cancelling a protection plan

You can cancel a protection plan within **15 days** of purchase for a full refund, as long as no claim has been made. If you return the product itself, the protection plan is cancelled and refunded automatically.

## Frequently asked questions

**I lost the physical invoice. Is my warranty still valid?** Yes. Download the invoice from My Orders at any time; it is accepted by brands as proof of purchase.

**The brand's service centre refused my claim. What can I do?** Ask the service centre for the reason in writing, then contact our support team with the job sheet. We will help you follow up with the brand.

**Is there a warranty on refurbished products?** Refurbished products come with the warranty shown on their product page, usually 6 months, provided by the refurbishing partner.
