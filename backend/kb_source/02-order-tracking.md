# Order Tracking

This article explains how to track your order, what each order status means, and what to do if tracking is delayed or an order shows as delivered but you have not received it.

## How to track your order

1. Go to **Account > My Orders**.
2. Select the order you want to track.
3. Tap **Track Order** to see the current status, the courier partner, the tracking ID, and the expected delivery date.

Once your order is shipped, we also send the tracking ID (also called the AWB number) and a tracking link by SMS, email, and WhatsApp (if you have opted in). You can use the tracking ID on the courier partner's website as well.

## What each order status means

- **Order Placed:** We have received your order and payment (or your COD request).
- **Confirmed:** The seller has accepted the order and it is being prepared.
- **Packed:** The item has been packed and is waiting to be picked up by the courier.
- **Shipped:** The courier has picked up the package. The tracking ID is now active.
- **In Transit:** The package is moving between courier hubs towards your city.
- **Out for Delivery:** The delivery agent has the package and will deliver it today.
- **Delivered:** The package has been handed over at your delivery address.
- **Delivery Attempted:** The courier tried to deliver but could not complete delivery. They will try again on the next working day.
- **Returned to Origin (RTO):** Delivery failed after all attempts and the package is going back to our warehouse.

## Tracking not updating

It is normal for tracking information to take **up to 24 hours** to appear after the status changes to Shipped, because the courier updates its system when the package is scanned at the first hub.

Packages also may not show new scans while they are travelling long distances between hubs. This is common for deliveries to other states and remote areas.

If your tracking has shown **no update for 3 days** (or **5 days** for remote areas such as the North-Eastern states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, and Lakshadweep), please contact customer support with your order ID. We will raise an escalation with the courier partner and update you within 24 to 48 hours.

## Delayed orders

The expected delivery date shown in My Orders is an estimate. Deliveries can be delayed because of:

- Heavy rain, floods, or other bad weather
- Festivals and sale events, when order volumes are very high
- Local restrictions, strikes, or road closures
- An incomplete or incorrect address or an unreachable phone number

If the expected delivery date has passed by more than 2 days, contact support. If your order is significantly delayed and you no longer need it, you can refuse the delivery when it arrives; prepaid orders are refunded once the package returns to our warehouse.

## Orders with multiple packages

If you ordered several items, they may be shipped separately from different warehouses or sellers. Each package has its own tracking ID. In **My Orders**, open the order to see all shipments and track each one individually. Receiving one package does not mean the others are missing.

## Delivery OTP

For orders above Rs. 5,000 and for some high-value items, a one-time delivery OTP is sent to your registered mobile number on the day of delivery. Share this OTP with the delivery agent only after you have received the package. The OTP confirms that the right person received the order.

## Order shows Delivered but not received

Sometimes a package is marked Delivered before it reaches you, or it is handed to a family member, neighbour, or security guard. Before reporting, please:

1. Check with family members, neighbours, the building reception, or security.
2. Check your mailbox or any safe place mentioned in your delivery instructions.
3. Look at the delivery details in Track Order, which may include a delivery photo or the name of the person who received it.
4. Wait 24 hours, as some couriers mark packages delivered a little early.

If you still have not received it, report it within **3 days** of the Delivered status by going to **My Orders > select the order > Help > Item not received**. We will investigate with the courier partner. Investigations take up to **5 business days**. If the package cannot be traced, we will send a replacement or give you a full refund.

## Changing the delivery address after shipping

The delivery address cannot be changed after an order is shipped. If you will not be available, you can contact the delivery agent using the number shared in the Out for Delivery message, or let the courier reattempt delivery on the next working day. Couriers make up to 3 delivery attempts.
