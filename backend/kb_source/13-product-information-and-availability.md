# Product Information & Availability

This article explains how to read a product page, find the right size, understand stock and availability, and what to do if a product is out of stock.

## What the product page tells you

Every product page includes:

- **Price:** The selling price, the MRP (Maximum Retail Price), and the discount. All prices are **inclusive of GST**.
- **Variants:** Available sizes, colours, storage capacities, or other options.
- **Delivery estimate:** Enter your pin code to see the expected delivery date and whether Cash on Delivery is available.
- **Specifications:** Technical details, materials, dimensions, and care instructions.
- **In the box:** Exactly what is included in the package.
- **Return Policy:** The return window and whether the item is returnable, exchangeable, or replacement-only.
- **Warranty:** The manufacturer's warranty period and service details.
- **Seller:** Who is selling the product.
- **Ratings, reviews, and Questions & Answers** from other customers.

## Sizes and fit

For clothing and footwear, tap **Size Chart** on the product page. It shows body and garment measurements in inches and centimetres for each size. Tips for choosing the right size:

- Compare the chart with a similar item you own that fits you well.
- Read the **Fit** note (for example, Slim Fit, Regular Fit, or Oversized).
- If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for a relaxed fit.
- Customer reviews often mention whether a product runs small or large.

If the size does not fit, fashion items can be exchanged for another size within 10 days. See the Product Exchange article.

## Colours and images

We try to show product colours accurately. However, the actual colour may look slightly different because of lighting during photography and your screen settings. Slight colour variation is not considered a wrong or defective product.

## Stock and availability

- **In Stock:** The product can be ordered and shipped.
- **Only X left:** Few units remain. Stock is not reserved when you add an item to your cart; it is reserved only after the order is placed.
- **Out of Stock:** The product cannot be ordered right now. Tap **Notify Me** to receive an SMS, email, or app notification when it is back in stock. Restocking is not guaranteed.
- **Not deliverable to your pin code:** The product is available but cannot be delivered to your location, usually because of its size or the seller's delivery coverage.

## Quantity limits

To make popular products available to as many customers as possible and to prevent reselling, some products have a **maximum quantity per order or per customer**, usually between 3 and 10 units. The limit is shown on the product page. For bulk or corporate orders of 10 or more units, see the General Customer Support FAQ.

## Pre-orders

Some new launches are available for pre-order. You pay when placing the pre-order, and the product is shipped on or after the release date shown on the product page. Pre-orders can be cancelled any time before shipping for a full refund. Cash on Delivery is not available for pre-orders.

## Sellers and genuine products

Products are sold either by us directly or by verified third-party sellers. The seller's name is shown on the product page. All sellers must follow our return, replacement, and delivery policies unless the product page states otherwise.

All products are sourced from brands or their authorised distributors. If you believe you received a counterfeit or fake product, report it immediately from **My Orders > Help > Report a problem**. These cases are handled as a priority by our support team.

## Price changes and pricing errors

Prices and offers can change at any time. The price at the time you place the order is final and will not change later. If a product was listed at a clearly incorrect price because of a technical error, we may cancel the order and refund you in full.

## Questions about a product

If the information you need is not on the product page:

- Check the **Questions & Answers** section, or post your own question. Questions are answered by the seller or other customers.
- Contact customer support for delivery, availability, and policy questions.

## Reviews and ratings

You can rate and review a product after it has been delivered. Reviews are published after moderation, which takes up to **48 hours**. Reviews that include offensive language, personal information, or links, or that are not about the product, are not published.
