# Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with TaskFlow. This article explains when refunds are available and how to request one.

## 30-day money-back guarantee (annual plans)

If you purchase a new **annual** Pro or Business subscription, you can request a full refund within **30 days** of the initial purchase date. This applies to your first annual purchase only. Renewals of an existing annual plan are eligible for a full refund within **7 days** of the renewal charge.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions are **not eligible for refunds**. When you cancel a monthly plan, you keep access to paid features until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. We do not provide partial refunds for unused days in a monthly cycle.

## Removed seats

When you remove members from your workspace, the unused portion of their seat is issued as a **prorated account credit**, not a cash refund. Credits are automatically applied to your next invoice and do not expire while your workspace is active.

## Downgrades

If you downgrade from Business to Pro, or from an annual plan to a monthly plan, the difference is issued as account credit. Downgrades from a paid plan to Free after the refund window has passed are not refunded; you retain paid features until the end of your current term.

## Non-refundable items

The following are not refundable:

- Charges older than the eligible refund window
- Add-ons such as extra storage packs after 7 days
- Enterprise contracts, which are governed by your signed order form
- Accounts terminated for violating our Terms of Service

## How to request a refund

1. Sign in as the workspace **owner** or **billing admin**.
2. Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**.
3. Click **Request refund** next to the eligible invoice. The button only appears if the invoice is within the refund window.
4. Choose a reason and submit.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank. If the original card has expired, your bank will usually forward the funds to your replacement card.

## Refund disputes and exceptions

If your refund request was denied, or you believe you were charged incorrectly (for example, a duplicate charge or a renewal after you cancelled), contact the billing team. Billing disputes are reviewed by a human agent, and we'll look at each case individually. Please avoid filing a chargeback with your bank while the dispute is open: a chargeback automatically suspends the workspace until it is resolved.

## Chargebacks

If a chargeback is filed, the workspace is suspended pending review. Once the chargeback is resolved, access can be restored after the outstanding balance is settled.
