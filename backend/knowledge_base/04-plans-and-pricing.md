# TaskFlow Plans and Pricing

TaskFlow offers four plans designed for individuals, growing teams, and large organizations. All prices are in US dollars and are listed per user (seat) per month.

## Free

- **Price:** $0
- Up to 5 members per workspace
- Up to 3 active projects
- 100 MB file storage per workspace
- Basic list and board views
- 7 days of activity history

The Free plan is ideal for trying TaskFlow or managing personal tasks.

## Pro

- **Price:** $10 per user/month billed annually, or $12 billed monthly
- Unlimited projects and members
- 10 GB storage per user
- Timeline (Gantt) and calendar views
- Integrations with Slack, Google Calendar, GitHub, and Zapier
- Custom fields and task templates
- Unlimited activity history

## Business

- **Price:** $20 per user/month billed annually, or $24 billed monthly
- Everything in Pro
- Advanced roles and permissions, including guest access controls
- Workload management and portfolio dashboards
- Enforced two-factor authentication
- Priority email support with a 4-hour response target on business days
- Audit log (90 days)

## Enterprise

- **Price:** Custom — contact sales
- Everything in Business
- SAML Single Sign-On and SCIM user provisioning
- Unlimited audit log retention
- Data residency options (US or EU)
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Dedicated customer success manager

## Free trial

Every new workspace can start a **14-day free trial** of the Business plan with no credit card required. At the end of the trial, the workspace automatically moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan. You won't lose any data, but features above the Free plan's limits will become read-only.

## Discounts

- **Annual billing** saves about 17% compared with monthly billing.
- **Nonprofits and education:** Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off Pro and Business. Apply from **Settings → Billing → Request a discount**.
- **Startups:** Companies under two years old with less than $1M in funding may qualify for our startup program.

## Taxes

Prices do not include sales tax, VAT, or GST, which are added at checkout based on your billing address. Business customers in the EU can enter a VAT ID to apply the reverse-charge mechanism.

For help choosing a plan, see "Upgrading Your Plan" or contact our sales team.
