# Upgrading Your Plan

Upgrading unlocks more features and higher limits for your whole workspace. Upgrades take effect immediately.

## Who can upgrade

Only the workspace **owner** or a member with the **Billing admin** role can change the plan. If you are a regular member and need a paid feature, click **Request upgrade** on any locked feature — this sends a notification to your workspace owner.

## How to upgrade

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**.
2. Choose Pro or Business, and select monthly or annual billing.
3. Review the number of seats. By default, this equals your current number of active members.
4. Enter or confirm your payment method.
5. Click **Upgrade now**.

You'll receive a confirmation email and an invoice immediately.

## How upgrade charges are calculated

Upgrades are **prorated**. If you upgrade partway through a billing period, you are charged only for the remaining days at the new rate, minus any unused credit from your current plan. For example, if you are halfway through a monthly Pro cycle and upgrade to Business, you'll pay roughly half of the Business monthly price, minus half of the Pro price you already paid.

Switching from **monthly to annual** billing starts a new 12-month term on the day you switch. Any unused monthly time is credited toward the annual charge.

## Upgrading from a free trial

If your workspace is in its 14-day Business trial, you can upgrade at any time. Your paid plan starts immediately; the remaining trial days are not added on. Nothing about your data or setup changes.

## Upgrading from Free

When you upgrade from the Free plan, all locked projects and read-only data become fully editable again. Integrations that were disabled due to plan limits will need to be reconnected from **Settings → Integrations**.

## Adding seats

You don't need to change plans to add people. Inviting new members automatically adds seats, charged on a prorated basis. Annual plans are billed for added seats at your next quarterly true-up, unless you prefer immediate billing, which you can choose under **Billing → Seat billing preference**.

## Moving to Enterprise

Enterprise plans are set up with our sales team because they include contracts, SSO configuration, and custom terms. Click **Contact sales** on the pricing page or in **Settings → Billing**. A representative typically replies within one business day. Your workspace will stay on its current plan until the Enterprise contract is signed, so there is no downtime during the transition.

## Upgrade didn't apply

If you upgraded but still see features locked, refresh the page or sign out and back in. Plan changes can take up to two minutes to reach all devices. If the issue persists, contact support with your invoice number.
