# Downgrading or Cancelling Your Subscription

You can downgrade or cancel your TaskFlow subscription at any time. This article explains what happens to your data and billing.

## Downgrading to a lower paid plan

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**.
2. Select the lower plan (for example, Business to Pro).
3. Review the list of features you'll lose.
4. Click **Confirm downgrade**.

Downgrades take effect at the **end of your current billing period**. Until then, you keep all current features. Any price difference is not refunded as cash but is issued as credit if applicable (see "Refund Policy").

## Cancelling a paid subscription

Cancelling moves your workspace to the **Free plan** at the end of your current billing period.

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription** at the bottom of the page.
3. Tell us why you're leaving (optional, but it helps us improve).
4. Confirm the cancellation.

You'll receive a confirmation email. Keep this email as proof of cancellation. If you are charged after cancelling, forward it to our billing team.

## What happens to your data

Cancelling or downgrading **never deletes your data**. However, features and content above the new plan's limits become **read-only**:

- If you have more than 3 active projects on Free, the most recently updated 3 stay active; others are archived and viewable but not editable.
- Timeline views, custom fields, and automations stop working but their configuration is saved.
- Integrations above your plan's limits are disconnected.
- Files over the storage limit remain downloadable but you can't upload new files.

If you re-upgrade later, everything is restored exactly as it was.

## Removing members before downgrading

The Free plan supports up to 5 members. If your workspace has more, the downgrade will still succeed, but only the owner and the four most recently active members keep edit access. Others become viewers. We recommend reviewing members in **Admin → Members** before downgrading.

## Reactivating a cancelled subscription

If you change your mind before your billing period ends, go to **Settings → Billing** and click **Resume subscription**. You won't be charged again until your normal renewal date.

## Cancelling vs. deleting

Cancelling your subscription is **not the same as deleting your account**. Your workspace remains on the Free plan and your data is kept. If you want your data permanently erased, see "Deleting Your Account or Workspace." Account deletion is irreversible and is handled by our support team for workspaces with paid history.
